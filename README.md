## Problem and Solution

### 1. Opinions create value, people don't capture it
- **Problem:** Billions of opinions are shared online every day; almost nobody is rewarded for them.
- **VOICE:** Voting and poll creation turn a user's judgement into an on-chain asset that earns from a daily reward pool.

### 2. Projects can't read their audience
- **Problem:** Teams have likes and comments, not structured data on what people think.
- **VOICE:** Aggregated opinions become a public consensus layer and signals projects can act on.

### 3. Campaigns buy clicks, not insight
- **Problem:** Quest and engagement campaigns produce activity but little usable data.
- **VOICE:** Custom campaign spaces where every interaction produces engagement and first-party data.

## How it works

1. **User wallet** — the user connects a wallet holding $VOICE.
2. **Active participation** — voting freezes tokens on the chosen option (a short take is optional); tokens collected on a poll form its reward pool.
3. **VOICE smart contract** — frozen and used tokens are held on-chain and partially form the daily reward pool.
4. **Ecosystem pool** — B2B payments and partner campaign budgets flow in from outside.
5. **Reward distribution** — the daily pool (participation + ecosystem + B2B) is distributed to participants automatically.

```
User ──▶ Freeze $VOICE & vote ──▶ VOICE smart contract ──┐
Poll creator ──▶ Poll reward pool ───────────────────────┤
Partners (B2B, campaigns) ──▶ Ecosystem pool ────────────┤
                                                         ▼
                                    Daily reward pool ──▶ Participants
                                                         │
                                    Aggregated opinions ──▶ On-chain data & signals
```

## Features

**B2C**
- Voting and poll creation — share judgements or collect opinions
- Social feed — comments, follows, followers

**B2B**
- On-chain data layer — opinions turned into public consensus and signals
- AI calibration — datasets to measure how an agent's decisions match human conviction
- Marktech infrastructure — custom spaces for acquisition, activation, onboarding and retention

**Pre-launch: Social Camp** — tasks, polls and referrals earn CLOUT → tiers, whitelist, allocation; Partnership Clusters for partner campaigns.

## Why Solana

- **Cost** — frequent small votes and daily payouts need sub-cent fees
- **Speed** — fast finality for high-volume voting
- **Composability** — on-chain opinion data readable by any Solana app, wallet or agent
